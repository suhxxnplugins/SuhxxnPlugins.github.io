Terms and Conditions

How I work with clients, in plain words. Please read this before you send a request.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Who these terms are for

These terms apply when you ask me ("I", "me", suhxxn plugins) to build a plugin or do other work. "You" is the person or server who asks. You must be 18 or over. By ticking the box on the request form, or by asking me to start work, you agree to these terms.

The request form is not a contract. Work starts only when the scope, price and date are agreed in writing. Messages on Discord or by email count as writing.

2. Requests and quotes

Asking for a quote is free. A quote is a fixed price for the scope agreed in writing. If the scope changes, I will give you a new quote before doing the extra work.

3. Payment

  • Deposit: 50% before I start. The deposit is what reserves my time for your project.
  • The rest: paid on delivery, before I release the final jar and source code.
  • The payment method and any other details are written into the quote.

4. Scope and revisions

The written scope says what the plugin does and does not do, your Minecraft version and the date. Two rounds of changes inside that scope are included. A change is something that fixes or adjusts what was agreed. New features, or a different idea of what the plugin should do, are quoted separately.

5. Delivery time

The delivery time is agreed in the written scope. It depends on you giving me the information and access I ask for in good time. If it is going to slip, I will tell you early.

6. What I need from you

  • Correct information: your exact Minecraft and Paper version and the other plugins you run.
  • Answers to my questions, and feedback on the revisions, in a reasonable time.
  • A backup of your server before you install anything I deliver. You are responsible for installing and running it on your server.
  • No passwords, tokens or other secrets sent to me in messages. If I need access to something, we agree how together.

7. Ownership of the code

  • Once you have paid in full, you own the finished plugin and its source code.
  • I keep the right to reuse generic parts that are not specific to you, such as helper code and common patterns. I never reuse your branding, settings, secrets or unique features.
  • Unless we agree otherwise, I will not publish your server's name, your code or your private details. I will ask first if I want to show the work.
  • Plugins use other software, such as the Paper API. Those pieces keep their own licenses.

8. Help after handover

For 14 days after handover I fix bugs in the agreed scope at no charge. This does not cover: breakage from a new Minecraft or Paper version, changes made by other plugins, problems with your server setup, or new features. I can quote those separately.

9. Cancellation and refunds

  • Before I start: you get the full deposit back.
  • After I have started: the deposit covers the work done so far. I send you what exists.
  • If I cannot deliver the agreed scope: you get the deposit back.

10. What I do not build

I do not build cheats, backdoors or malware, anything that harms other players or servers, or anything I cannot test reliably. If I stop a project for one of these reasons, I refund the part of the deposit that covers work I did not do.

11. How I work, and AI tools

I use AI tools as part of how I work. I test what I deliver on a real server, and I am the one who answers for the result.

12. No guarantees, and limits on my responsibility

I test my work, but no software is free of bugs. I deliver what the written scope says. My responsibility for any claim about a project is limited to the amount you paid me for that project. I am not responsible for lost players, lost income or lost data. That is why you take a backup first.

13. Changes to these terms

I may update these terms. Each project follows the version that was current on the day its scope was agreed. The date at the top shows the latest update.

14. Questions

Ask me before you start, not after.

Privacy Policy

What this website does with your information. Short answer: very little.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short. The request form runs in your browser and sends nothing to any server. I only receive your request if you send it to me yourself.
In short. Nothing is sent until you press the send button. Then your request goes to me, and only to me, through a small relay. This page has no analytics, cookies or trackers.

1. The request form

The form runs in your browser. This page does not send what you type to any server. When you tick the agreement box and press the button, the page turns your answers into a text message on your screen. Nothing has been sent until you copy that message and send it to me.

Nothing you type is sent while you fill in the form. When you tick the agreement box and press send, the page sends your answers to a small relay that I run (a Google Apps Script web app). The relay posts them to a private channel on my Discord server. The page's security settings allow it to connect only to Google's script servers, where the relay runs, and nowhere else. If sending fails, the page tells you and shows a message you can copy and send to me yourself.

2. What is saved on your device

The last 20 requests you create are saved in this browser's own storage, on your device, so you can copy them again. They are not sent to me. The Remove button deletes one request, and clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them all. I cannot see them.

3. Hosting, fonts, cookies and tracking

  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Those two services can see ordinary visit details, such as your IP address and browser type, the same as for any web page. Their own privacy policies apply to that.
  • This page adds no analytics, no cookies and no trackers.
  • When you press send, your request passes through Google's servers (the relay) on its way to Discord. The relay does not store your request. It only remembers the request ID for a few hours, so the same request is not posted twice. Google keeps its usual technical logs. Discord stores the message in my private channel, under its own policies.

4. What I receive

I receive only what you choose to send me, on Discord.I receive what you enter in the form and send. If you also write to me on Discord or by email, I receive that too. That can include your Discord name, your server name, what you want built, your deadline and your budget, and the line showing that you agreed to the terms and when. It is also handled by Discord or your email provider, under their own policies.

5. How I use it

  • To reply to you, give you a quote and carry out the project.
  • To keep a record of what we agreed, including that you accepted the terms.
  • I do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • I use AI tools in my work, so details of your project may be processed by them while I work on it. Do not send me passwords, tokens or other secrets.

6. How long I keep it

I keep what you send for as long as the project lasts, and afterwards for as long as I need a record of the agreement. You can ask me to delete it, and I will, unless I need to keep something for a legal or accounting reason.

7. Age

This website and my services are for people aged 18 or over, and the form asks you to confirm it. They are not meant for children.

8. Your choices

  • Delete the requests saved in your browser, as described in section 2.
  • Ask me to show you or delete what you sent me.
